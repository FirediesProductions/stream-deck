# Rank Showcase

**Earn it. Show it.**

You climbed for that rank. It shouldn't live buried three menus deep in a launcher. Rank Showcase
puts it on a key as an engraved plaque - your badge, your tier, your number - so it's on your desk
and in your stream, permanently.

Type it in and it stays. Connect an account and it updates itself.

## Quick start

1. Drag **Rank Showcase** (under **Rank Showcase**) onto any key or dial.
2. Pick a badge and type your rank. That's a finished key.
3. If your game is one we can read, switch to **Account** and it keeps itself current.

## Sixty badges

**Five finishes** - each drawn as a real material rather than a flat colour - across **twelve
tiers**, from Wooden up through Iron, Bronze, Silver, Gold, Diamond and Ruby to Legendary Radiant.
Sixty in all.

The tier names are only the badges' names. Nothing forces your rank to match one: put **Immortal**,
**Grandmaster**, **Level 412** or your guild's own word on any badge you like.

Or use **your own image**. Drop in a PNG and the plaque builds itself around that instead - the
answer for a game we don't cover, a private league, or a badge you made yourself.

## Your own ladder

Playing something we don't read? Build the ladder yourself. Name each tier in your own words, choose
the badge that goes with it, and the key steps up as you type a new rank - exactly as the built-in
ones do. Guild ranks, a private league, a nickname system among friends: if it has an order, it
works.

## Live from your account

**Every game works. Seven update themselves.**

Whatever you play, you can type your rank in and it stays there - no list to be on, nothing to break
when a service changes. That's the whole product working, for everyone.

These seven can also fetch it for you, so the key keeps itself current without you touching it:

| Service | What it reads |
|---|---|
| **Chess.com** | Your rating, in whichever time control you pick. |
| **Lichess** | The same, from the other side of the board. |
| **Dota 2** | Your medal, via OpenDota. |
| **Old School RuneScape** | Total level, straight from the hiscores. |
| **RuneScape 3** | The same, for the other one. |
| **Tibia** | Character level. |
| **Guild Wars 2** | WvW rank. This one needs an API key from your account. |

Enter your name, press **Fetch now**, and the key looks after itself from then on. The button spins
while it's working, so you always know whether it's fetching or finished.

## The colour comes off the badge

The header, the number and the plaque don't have their own palette. They're taken from whatever
badge the key is wearing - so a gold badge gives you a gold face, and the moment you climb a tier
the whole key moves with you.

That holds for **your own image** too. Upload a badge and the colour is read out of it, which is why
a custom plaque looks made rather than assembled.

Prefer to drive it yourself? **Custom** takes one colour of your choosing and runs it through the
same ladder. And if you want the last word, every element - header, number, the live dot - can be
set by hand, with a reset that puts it all back.

## The face is yours

- **What sits in the middle** - the badge, or the number. A rating of 2140 deserves the centre; a
  Radiant badge probably deserves it more.
- **Your header and plaque text** - the game above, the league below, or whatever you want them to
  say.
- **The live dot** - an optional corner light: off, left, centre or right. On a live account it shows the account's own status where the service reports one - green for online.
- **A tactile click on press**, with your own sound and volume if you want them.
- **It renders in your own script** - Japanese, Chinese, Korean, Arabic, Hindi, Thai, Hebrew and
  more, so a guild name doesn't have to be Latin to fit.

## Key and dial

Both, laid out differently on purpose. A key is a square, so the badge leads and the rank sits under
it. A dial is a wide strip with room for a sentence, so the badge moves left and your name becomes
the hero, the game beneath it, the league in the accent.

It isn't the same face stretched - a 400 × 200 strip can carry a hierarchy a 240 × 240 square
cannot, so it carries a different one.

## Honest limits

- **Seven services, not every game.** These are the ones with a public way to ask. If yours isn't
  listed, type your rank or build your own ladder - both look identical on the key, and neither can
  break when a service changes.
- **A live rank is only as fresh as its service.** Ladders update on their own schedule, and a match
  you just won may not be reflected for a while. That's their cadence, not the key lagging.
- **Guild Wars 2 needs an API key** that you generate in your own account. It's read-only and it
  stays on your machine.
- **Nothing about you is sent anywhere.** The only requests made are to the ladder you asked for,
  with the name you typed. No account, no analytics, no telemetry.
- **An uploaded badge is used as given.** We read its colour and build the plaque around it - we
  don't recolour, crop or redraw your image.
- **Windows only, for now.**
